About

Designing Play Through Systems and Iteration

I have worked across AAA games, independent teams, and digital-physical product development. My work includes LIMBO and INSIDE at Playdead, mission and systems work at Studio Gobo, and seven years at the LEGO Group with LEGO Super Mario and SMART Play experimentation.

My background combines game design, level design, scripting, technical collaboration, and playtesting. I focus on building systems that are understandable for teams, scalable for production, and clear for players.

  • Core strengths: systems design, mission design, visual scripting, cross-discipline communication
  • Recent highlights: LEGO Super Mario tooling and memory optimization, SMART Play prototyping
  • Selected shipped titles: LIMBO, INSIDE, For Honor Arcade Mode
  • Technical focus: gameplay scripting, event architecture, workflow tooling
Approach

Rapid Prototyping With Production Constraints

I work in short iteration loops with players and stakeholders, then convert insights into concrete design and technical changes. The recurring pattern across projects has been to simplify systems for designers while keeping implementation robust enough for long-term growth.
